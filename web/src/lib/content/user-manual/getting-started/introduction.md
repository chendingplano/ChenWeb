# Introduction

ChenWeb is a knowledge management and document review platform. It ingests documents, extracts structured metrics, and helps reviewers find gaps, inconsistencies, and missing information across large sets of technical and standards documents.

This manual covers how to find your way around the app. Use the tree on the left to browse sections; click a page to read it here on the right.

## What you can do in ChenWeb

- **Import documents** into the Knowledge Store and track their processing status.
- **Review extracted metrics** against source documents and flag issues.
- **Browse metadata** such as document structure, chunks, and inputs.
- **Manage videos and other resources** used for training and reference.

Keep reading in **Getting Started → Installation** for how to get a local environment running, or jump to **Navigating the Dashboard** to understand the app's layout.
