# Navigating the Dashboard

The main application shell has three areas:

- **Left rail** — the primary navigation menu, grouped by workspace area (Knowledge Base, Applications, System Admin, Personal, and Resources).
- **Content panel** — the main working area in the middle. Whatever you select in the left rail renders here.
- **Right shelf** — contextual information related to what's open in the content panel. You can collapse it to get more room.

## Resizing panels

Both the left rail and the right shelf have a drag handle on their inner edge (a thin vertical strip that highlights on hover). Click and drag it to make the panel wider or narrower.

## The Resources area

This page — the User's Manual — lives under **Resources → Documents**. It has its own two-panel layout, independent from the outer app shell: a tree of manual pages on the left, and the selected page's content on the right, also with its own drag-to-resize divider.

See **Resources** for what else is available in this area.
