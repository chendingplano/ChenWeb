You review one row extracted from a technical standard. The row is a requirement that names
something but gives no value: the document leaves the value open. Decide what the open value
belongs to.

object_quantity: the open value is a quantity of a thing, facility, vehicle, container,
equipment, product, material, sample or test: something with a unit, count or scale (an amount,
size, volume, capacity, mass, concentration, rate, energy use, duration of a process applied to
the object, number of items or staff to provide). The document leaves it to be declared by a
supplier or manufacturer, agreed between named parties, or set, sized, chosen or matched
according to another factor (throughput, population, demand, site conditions) or by design.
Examples: 设备应明确比能耗 (specific energy consumption, to be declared); 应根据垃圾日处理量合理设置
单室体积 (chamber volume, sized by throughput); 抗压强度由供需双方商定 (compressive strength, agreed);
应根据垃圾的类别、数量等要求配备收集设备数量 (number of vehicles to provide); 浸提时间由双方约定 (extraction
time of a test).

activity_schedule: the open value is when, how often or how an activity is carried out
(collection, transport, cleaning, inspection, maintenance, publicity, drop-off), and the document
only tells parties to agree, set or announce it. The duty is organizational; there is no object
with a property to measure. Examples: 收运单位应与集中供餐单位约定餐厨垃圾收运的时间和频次 (agree the
collection time and frequency); 检修计划应提前公告 (announce the maintenance plan); 公告投放时间、地点、方式
(announce drop-off time, place and method).

not_a_quantity: the row names a method, process, practice, feature, record or identity, not a
quantity (主体工艺, a treatment route, a management system, a species name, "has a lid").

Judge the clause in `context`, not the wording of the row's name: the extractor may have named an
activity's schedule as if it were a quantity, or vice versa.
