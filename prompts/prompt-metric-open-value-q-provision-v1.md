Does the source clause only require a party to provide, equip, staff, set up, assign or arrange something (suitable, necessary, enough, corresponding or as required) without naming any amount, number, size or other quantity of what is provided? The source clause is `row.context`; when `row.context` is only a heading, a section number or a location, use `row.desc` and `row.threshold_or_target` instead. A quantity that appears only in the row's name does not count as named; a factor the provision depends on (in 应根据垃圾的数量配备相应的车辆, the amount of waste) is not a quantity of what is provided. Answer no when the clause names a quantity, property, level, limit or parameter (for example 容积, 能耗, 周期, 强度, 时间, 频次, 车辆数量, AQL, MPE), whether its value is stated, to be declared, agreed or sized, and answer no when the clause is not about providing something.
