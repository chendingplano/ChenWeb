Does the source clause itself name a quantity of the thing the row is about (the equipment, staff, facility, product, sample, test or activity that must have the value): an amount, size, volume, capacity, duration, energy use, frequency, level, error, limit, count or number of it (for example 车辆数量, 人数, 台数, 容积, 周期, 时间, 频次, 能耗, 强度, 能力, AQL, MPE)? The source clause is `row.context`; when `row.context` is only a heading, a section number or a location, use `row.desc` and `row.threshold_or_target` instead. Words that name a factor the value depends on do not count: in 应根据垃圾的数量配备相应的车辆, 数量 is the amount of waste, not of the vehicles, so the answer is no. Answer no when the clause only says to provide, equip, staff or do something suitable, necessary or as required, and the quantity appears only in the row's name.
