## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Product Review is reachable from the Applications menu
The Applications section of the workspace navigation SHALL include a "Product Review" entry that
opens the Product Review intake page. This SHALL be the only way most users discover the app —
no direct link or administrator hand-off SHALL be required.

#### Scenario: Applications menu lists Product Review
- **WHEN** a user opens the Applications section of the navigation
- **THEN** a "Product Review" entry is listed among its children

#### Scenario: Selecting Product Review opens the intake page
- **WHEN** a user selects the "Product Review" entry
- **THEN** the Product Review intake page opens
