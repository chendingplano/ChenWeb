## Prompts
All prompts should be saved in `prompts`. File names must be prefixed with `prompt-` and endded with 
`-v<number>.md` (for markdown) or `_v<number>.txt` for other types. Example: `prompt-for-my-test-v1.md. Example: `prompt-for-my-test-v1.md`
