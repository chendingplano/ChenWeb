Edge / Connection Style

How should edges (wires between nodes) look and route?

A — Smooth Bezier

Curved S-shaped connections. Elegant, organic feel. The style used in n8n, Freepik Spaces, and most modern flow editors.

B — Orthogonal (Step)

Right-angle routes that avoid overlapping nodes. Technical, precise feel. Common in UML and circuit diagram tools.

C — Straight Line

Direct point-to-point connections. Simplest to implement and reason about. Works well for sparse, clear graphs.
